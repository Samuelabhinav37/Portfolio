## Lessons for defenders

A closing section, checklist, or takeaways — plain Markdown lists work fine:

- **Lockfile-only installs** — install exactly what's been pinned and verified.
- **Egress baselining** — know what your build pipeline normally talks to.
- **Hardware MFA** — for every account with publish permission, no carve-outs.
